About Newcastle Musicians

A free directory for the Hunter Valley music community.

Newcastle Musicians is a free, independent directory connecting local bands and solo artists with event bookers, venue managers, wedding planners and music lovers across Newcastle and the Hunter Valley.

Why We Built This

Finding good local live music shouldn't be hard. Newcastle has an incredible depth of talent — rock bands, jazz trios, folk singer-songwriters, electronic acts — but there's no central place to find them. We built this to fix that.

What We Offer

  • Free artist profiles with genre tags, photos, music embeds and booking contacts
  • Direct booking — contact artists without middlemen or commission
  • Filter by genre to find the right sound for any occasion
  • A place for local artists to be discovered by people who need them

For Artists

Listing your act is completely free. Submit your profile via the List Your Act page. We review all submissions and aim to publish approved profiles within 2 business days. To update your listing or remove it, contact us at the email below.

For Event Bookers

Browse the directory, check out each artist's music, and contact them directly via their booking email. No platform fees, no cuts — just direct connections.

Contact

Got a question, want to update your listing, or interested in partnering? Reach us at hello@newcastlemusicians.com.au
